## 2. Cookie Policy (GDPR Compliance)

This project may use cookies to improve the user experience. In compliance with GDPR regulations:  

### Types of Cookies
- **Technical/Essential Cookies:** Required for basic functionality and performance of the software or website.  
- **Preference Cookies:** Store your consent and settings preferences locally.  

### Consent Management
- Your consent for cookies is stored in your browser’s local storage.  
- You have the right to **withdraw consent at any time** by clearing browser cookies or adjusting your settings.  

### Control and Transparency
- No personal data is sent to external servers through cookies.  
- Users can control cookie settings at any time without impacting core software functionality.

## 3. Data Protection & Privacy

### No Personal Data Collection
- This software does **not collect any personally identifiable information (PII)** such as names, emails, or phone numbers.  

### Local Data Processing
- Any settings, progress, or local data are stored **on the user’s device only**.  
- No external servers or third parties receive this data.  

### User Rights
- You can delete all local data at any time.  
- Since no personal data is collected, GDPR rights such as access, correction, or erasure are inherently protected.
